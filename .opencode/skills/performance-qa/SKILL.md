# Performance QA

Do not call a product fast without a threshold and a measurement.

Use `studio-browser-check` with `performance` for the preview. If the mission has no performance target, choose a conservative development target as an assumption and publish that assumption in the Mission Contract.

Repair clear regressions and rerun the same measurement. Do not convert a single local measurement into a broad production guarantee.
