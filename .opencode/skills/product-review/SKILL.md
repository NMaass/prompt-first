# Reviewing Your Product

After the builder creates or updates your app, it's your job to review what was built. You're checking whether the product matches what you described in the spec and whether it actually works well for users.

## Review Checklist

### 1. Does each feature work?
Go through the acceptance criteria in your spec one by one. Try each feature in the preview:
- Tap every button — does it do what you expect?
- Fill in every input — does the data appear where it should?
- Navigate between screens — can you get everywhere you need to go?
- Try the main user flow from start to finish

### 2. Does it look right?
Check the visual design against your spec:
- Does the layout feel organized or cluttered?
- Are the most important things easy to find?
- Does the style match the mood you described (calm? energetic? professional?)
- Is text readable? Are buttons big enough to tap?

### 3. What happens at the edges?
Test unusual situations:
- What does the app look like with no data yet? (first-time user)
- What happens if you enter nothing and tap submit?
- What happens with very long text?
- What does it look like on a small screen?

### 4. Is anything confusing?
Pretend you're a new user seeing this for the first time:
- Would you know what to do first?
- Are labels and button text clear?
- Is it obvious what each screen is for?

## How to Give Feedback

### Describe the problem, not the solution

**Less helpful:** "Move the button to the top"
**More helpful:** "I keep missing the main action — it doesn't stand out enough"

**Less helpful:** "Make the text bigger"
**More helpful:** "The habit names are hard to read at a glance"

### Be specific about where

**Less helpful:** "Something looks off"
**More helpful:** "On the home screen, the list of habits feels too crowded — there's no breathing room between items"

### Prioritize

Don't try to fix everything at once. Pick the 2-3 most important issues and address those first. You can always do another round of improvements after.

### Reference the spec

Connect your feedback to the spec: "The spec says users should see their weekly progress, but I don't see that anywhere on the home screen"
